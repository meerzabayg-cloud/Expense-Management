# Smart Family Expense & Grocery Manager: User Guide

This guide is written for everyday users. You don't need any technical knowledge to follow it.

---

## 1. What this app does

- **Grocery lists.** Plan your monthly shopping, tick items off in the shop, and see the estimated cost.
- **Receipt scanning.** Take a photo of a receipt. The phone reads it, you check the result, and it is saved.
- **Expenses.** Record all spending (rent, fuel, school, dining…) and money received.
- **Funds and budgets.** Track cash, bank and card balances, and set monthly limits.
- **Price comparison.** See whether rice, milk and other items got cheaper or more expensive, and at which store.
- **Reports.** View monthly and yearly summaries, charts, and CSV export for Excel.

**Your data stays on your phone.** The app has no sign-up, no cloud and no ads.

## 2. Installing the app (one time, about one minute)

You need the app link, for example `https://<your-github-name>.github.io/Expense-Management/`.

### iPhone

1. Open the link in **Safari**. If you tapped it inside WhatsApp, first choose "Open in Safari".
2. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
4. From now on, open the app from the new **Family Budget** icon.

> Always use the home-screen icon. On iPhone, the home-screen app keeps its data separately from Safari.

### Android

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install app** when the banner appears, or open the ⋮ menu and tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Open the app from its new icon.

The first time the app opens, it downloads everything it needs, including the receipt scanner (about 7 MB). After that it **works without internet**.

---

## 3. First-time setup

1. Tap **Get started**.
2. Enter your name (optional) and your currency. **AED** is the default.
3. Optionally enter how much cash you have and your bank balance. You can skip this step.
4. Optionally set a monthly grocery budget and an overall budget.
5. Tap **Finish setup**.

## 4. Everyday use

### Planning the month's groceries
1. Tap **Grocery** at the bottom of the screen, then **New grocery list**.
2. Choose **From last month's purchases & list**. The app fills in what you bought last month along with the latest prices.
3. To change a quantity, tap the item. To add more items, tap **Add item**.
4. The top of the list shows the **estimated total** and how much **grocery budget is left**.

### In the supermarket
- Tick each item as you put it in the trolley.
- You can type the price you paid. This builds your price history.
- Ticking an item **does not** record an expense by itself. The expense is recorded when you scan the receipt.

### Scanning a receipt
1. Tap the green **Scan** button.
2. Tap **Take photo**. Lay the receipt flat in good light and fill the screen with it.
3. Wait a few seconds. The text is read on your phone and nothing is uploaded.
4. **Check everything.** Lines shown in yellow were hard to read. The box at the bottom tells you whether the lines add up to the total.
5. Tap **Confirm & save**. The expense, the price history and your grocery list are all updated together.

If a receipt can't be read, tap **Enter a receipt manually**. You can enter just the total, or the individual items.

### Recording other expenses
Tap **Expenses**, then **Add expense**. Enter the amount, category and store, then tap **Save**. To record salary, reimbursements or gifts, use **Money in**.

### Checking prices
Tap the ☰ menu, then **Price Comparison**. You can also tap any item. For each item you will see:
- the latest price, store and date, and the previous price, store and date
- how much more or less you paid, in AED and as a percentage
- the lowest, average and best-store price

Different pack sizes are compared fairly by price per kg, litre or piece.

### Monthly review
Open **Dashboard** or **Reports**. Use the arrows at the top to change the month. Green means good (under budget or a lower price) and red means overspending or a price increase.

## 5. Backups (important)

Your data is stored only on this phone. You lose it if:
- the phone is lost, broken or reset,
- you delete the app or clear the browser data, or
- the phone runs very low on storage and the system clears website data.

**Make a backup every week or two:**
1. Tap ☰ → **Backup & Restore**.
2. Choose a password and **write it down**.
3. Tap **Create protected backup**, then save the file to iCloud Drive or Google Drive, or email it to yourself.

> ⚠ Nobody can recover a forgotten backup password, including you. The file is strongly encrypted.

The app reminds you when it's time for a new backup.

### Moving to a new phone
1. Old phone: create a protected backup.
2. New phone: install the app, then on the welcome screen tap **I have a backup to restore**.
3. Choose the backup file and enter the password.

---

## 6. Sharing with family and friends

Send them the same link (☰ → **Help & Guide** → **Share link**). Each person installs their own copy, and **their data stays separate from yours**. Nothing is shared or synchronised. If you want a family member to see your figures, you can export a CSV report and send it to them.

---

## 7. Good to know

- **Balances are your own records.** They are calculated from what you enter and are not connected to your bank. Use **Adjust balance** if they drift.
- **Credit cards:** spending on a card increases what you owe. The card limit is never counted as your money. Paying the card from your bank is a **Transfer** and is not counted as spending again.
- **Recurring payments** (rent, utility bills, subscriptions) are suggested on the dashboard when due. You tap **Record** or **Skip**.
- **Updates:** when a new version is available, a bar appears with an **Update now** button.
- **Privacy:** the app contains no trackers or advertising, and it doesn't send your data anywhere.
